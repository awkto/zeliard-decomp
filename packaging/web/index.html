The original graphics drivers. Chosen at start; no key switches it in game.
Shows all 36 live columns of a cavern instead of 28. Boss rooms, towns and menus keep the original frame. In game: W.
The two zoom-out levels only apply with the wide cavern view on; otherwise the frame is shown whole. In game: Z (cropped, wide, zoom-out 1, zoom-out 2 with the wide view on; crop on or off without it).
Slides the scroll and sprites between game steps. In game: T.
In game: F or F11.
Gameplay
The same digits as the in-game speed menu: 9 is fastest, 0 slowest, 5 is the original. In game: F9 or Backspace, then a digit. Hold Shift to fast-forward.
Audio
In game: F1.
In game: F2. The port has no volume setting; use your device volume.
Controls
Auto hides them again when you use a keyboard or controller.
Settings are remembered in this browser, and keys pressed in game update them.
Controls: every keyboard shortcut

Saves (.usr), save states and settings are kept in this browser (IndexedDB), along with the game files you picked, so you only choose them once.
